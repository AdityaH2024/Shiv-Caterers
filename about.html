Our Story

Founded on the Belief That Great Food Is the Heart of Every Celebration

Shiv Caterers was established by Kashiram Supekar with a simple but powerful conviction — every family deserves hygienic, freshly prepared, and genuinely delicious food at their most meaningful moments.

What started as a modest catering venture in Chinchwad has grown into one of Pune's most trusted names for pure vegetarian event catering. From intimate housewarming ceremonies to large 500-guest weddings, we bring the same care, attention, and warmth to every event we serve.

Our deep roots in Maharashtrian culture mean we understand the sanctity of occasions like munj, dohale jevan, barsa, and vastu shanti — and we take pride in serving food that honours these traditions authentically.
